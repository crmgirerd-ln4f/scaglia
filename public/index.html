On the circuit without a connection

Once this page is open it needs no connection: everything is saved on this device, encrypted. Keep the tab open and do not reload it while offline. Before you leave the hotel, print the round sheet (Share and print) and export a backup. When the connection returns, team sync resumes on its own and the header shows it.

This device was opened with a new team code. What it held before is still here, encrypted with the previous code. Enter that code to merge it back; your edits win where they differ.
